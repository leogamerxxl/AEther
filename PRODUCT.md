# PRODUCT.md - AETHER

register: product

## What it is
Vertical spatial economic-intelligence OS for hospitality (pilot: Hotel Terra Neptun,
Romanian Black Sea coast). One explorable world model - a Mapbox dark globe down to the
hotel - where every surface answers one of the 8 product questions: What changed? Why does
it matter? Where? Who is affected? What should we do? How confident? What evidence?
Did the action work?

## Who uses it
The hotel owner, at 07:00, on a laptop, deciding tonight's and this week's pricing.
Romanian-language, evidence-first, zero fabricated numbers (Truth Doctrine: honest
loading / empty / degraded states; SAMPLE is always labeled).

## Surfaces (5 modes over one world)
- Harta (map, default): altitude bands globe->twin, live building glow, IO pins, drawer.
- Observa: pipeline health (agent_runs / daily_briefs) - is the system alive?
- Brief: the exact rendered_html morning brief, in-app.
- Decizii: the decision ledger - accept/dismiss + market verdicts (outcomes).
- Simulare: honest P6 stub until enough outcomes exist to learn elasticity.
